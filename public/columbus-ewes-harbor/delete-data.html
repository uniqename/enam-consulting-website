Delete Account & Data

How to request account deletion and data removal

How to Delete Your Account

Columbus Ewes Harbor provides you with full control over your personal data. You can request to delete your account and associated data at any time.

Steps to Request Account Deletion

1 Open the App
Go to your account settings within the Columbus Ewes Harbor mobile app.

2 Navigate to Settings
Tap the menu icon and select "Settings" from the navigation menu.

3 Select Delete Account
Look for the "Delete Account" option in the settings menu and tap it.

4 Confirm Deletion
Follow the prompts to confirm your deletion request. You may be asked to enter your password for security purposes.

5 Verify via Email
Check your email for a confirmation message. Click the link to complete the deletion process.

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, the following information is permanently removed:

  • Your profile information (name, email, phone number)
  • Your membership status and subscription data
  • Your event registrations and attendance records
  • Your payment history (though transaction records may be retained for 7 years for legal/tax purposes)
  • Your personal preferences and settings
  • Any messages you've sent

What Gets Retained

The following information may be retained after deletion for legal and operational reasons:

  • Financial records: Transaction records are retained for 7 years as required by tax law
  • Legal records: Information required to comply with legal obligations
  • Backup data: May exist in backups for up to 30 days

Alternative: Request Data Deletion Only

If you want to keep your account but delete specific data, you can request selective data deletion by contacting our support team at the email below. Requests will be processed within 30 days.

Need Help?

If you have trouble deleting your account through the app or need additional support, please contact us:

Email: privacy@columbusewesharbor.org

Support: support@columbusewesharbor.org

Data Deletion Timeline

  • Immediate: Your account will be deactivated and no longer visible to other members
  • 24-48 hours: Personal data is removed from active systems
  • 30 days: All data is permanently deleted from backups and logs
  • 7 years: Transaction records kept for tax/legal purposes, then deleted
