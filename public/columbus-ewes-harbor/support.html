Frequently Asked Questions

How do I log in?

Open the app and enter the phone number and PIN that the association has registered for you. If you are not sure of your PIN, contact an executive member or email us at support@columbusewesharbor.org.

How do I pay my dues?

Open the Dues section of the app to see your current balance, due date, and payment history. Follow the payment instructions shown on the screen. Payment records are updated by the treasurer once a payment is confirmed.

How do I RSVP for an event?

Open the Events section, choose an event, and tap the RSVP or Register button. You can see the headcount and your own RSVP status on the event page.

How do I sign up for a potluck dish?

Open the Potluck section for the event you are attending, choose a category and dish type, and tap to claim a slot. Your commitment will show on the event page.

How do I update my profile?

Open your profile from the main menu and edit your details. Changes are saved to your member record right away.

The app is not loading. What should I do?

  1. Close and reopen the app
  2. Check your internet connection
  3. Update the app to the latest version from the App Store or Google Play
  4. Restart your device

If the problem continues, contact us at support@columbusewesharbor.org.

How do I delete my account?

Visit our Account and Data Deletion page or email us at support@columbusewesharbor.org.
