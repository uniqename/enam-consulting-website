Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Columbus Ewes Harbor ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App") and related services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide you with community management features, including:

  • Member Information: Name, email address, phone number, family relationships, and membership status
  • Account Data: Account credentials, profile information, and preferences
  • Activity Information: Event RSVPs, dues payment status, task assignments, and member interactions
  • Payment Information: Payment references, transaction records, and payment history (payment processing handled securely)
  • Communications: Messages, announcements, and notifications within the platform
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and mobile advertising ID (for notifications)
  • Technical Information: IP addresses, access logs, and usage analytics

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for:

  • Providing core App functionality (member directory, event management, dues tracking, etc.)
  • Sending push notifications for events, dues reminders, and announcements
  • Processing membership dues and event payments
  • Generating community insights and analytics
  • Improving App features and user experience
  • Communicating with members about their accounts and the platform
  • Complying with legal obligations

4. Data Security

Your data is protected with industry-standard security measures:

  • Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Encrypted storage using Supabase (Google Cloud infrastructure)
  • No storage of sensitive financial data (credit card processing handled securely by third parties)
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Limited access to data (only authorized personnel)

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: Database and authentication provider (Google Cloud-hosted)
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: Push notification service (Google)
  • Payment Processors: For secure payment processing (details provided separately)

These services have their own privacy policies governing their use of your data.

6. Sharing of Information

We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We only share information:

  • Within the organization (visible to authorized admin members)
  • With third-party service providers necessary to operate the App
  • When required by law or legal process
  • To protect our rights, privacy, safety, or property

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Data Portability: Request your data in a portable format
  • Opt-Out: Disable push notifications in app settings

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion, we retain data only as required by law for financial and legal records (typically 7 years for transaction records).

9. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for adult members of organizations. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware of such collection, we will delete the data immediately.

10. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the App following changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
